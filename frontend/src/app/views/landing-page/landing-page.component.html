On the following pages you will go through several questionnaires and observe some images. Each image will be shown for a short duration after which you will be asked to enter tags, which in your opinion, describe the image. This process is called image tagging and helps us to improve image classification.

Participation will take approximately 20 minutes. Information on how we use the collected data can be found in the data privacy statement. Collected data will be transmitted completely anonymized and cannot be related back to you as a person.

Purpose of this study

This study investigates the effects of sound in gamification. Gamification describes the use of game elements in non-game contexts. This is usually done to increase user motivation. E.g., going for a run can be rewarded with Points, or reaching certain milestones can be rewarded with Badges.

To investigate the effectiveness of sound, participants are assigned to one of four conditions upon starting the study:

  1. No Gamification
  2. Gamification
  3. Gamification + Sound
  4. Sound Only

Based on this setup, participants will perform the image tagging task with either no game element at all (1.), Points and Badges which are rewarded and unlocked for tags (2. & 3.) and / or sound (3. & 4.) In the evaluation, conclusions about the effectiveness of sound can be drawn based on the amount of tags submitted by users in each condition among other factors.
